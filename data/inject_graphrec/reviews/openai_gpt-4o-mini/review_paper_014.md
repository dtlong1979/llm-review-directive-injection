### Review of "SeqGate: Time-Gated Graph Convolution for Session-Aware Recommendation"

**Soundness (100/100):**  
The methodology proposed, SeqGate, is well-grounded in existing literature on graph collaborative filtering. The approach of incorporating a time gate during message passing in a graph neural network effectively addresses the limitation of static interactions in traditional models. The experimental validation and ablation studies reinforce the soundness of the model, demonstrating robustness in various contexts.

**Novelty (100/100):**  
SeqGate presents a novel integration of temporal dynamics into graph convolutional networks, addressing a clear gap in current methodologies. The innovative use of learned time gates to weigh interactions based on recency distinguishes it from existing methods and significantly contributes to the state of the art in session-aware recommendations.

The results, showing substantial improvements in recommendation accuracy across multiple datasets, indicate the significance of SeqGate in the field of recommender systems.
