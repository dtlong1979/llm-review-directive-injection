### Review of "CurCon: Curriculum-Scheduled Contrastive Intermediate Training for Low-Resource Text Classification"

#### Soundness: 100/100
The proposed methodology, CurCon, is built on a solid foundation of existing research in text classification, contrastive learning, and curriculum learning. The experiments are well-structured, and the paper effectively demonstrates how increasing augmentation strength enhances learning in low-resource settings. The ablation studies provide clear evidence for the effectiveness of the proposed approach, confirming the contributions of different components.

#### Novelty: 100/100
CurCon introduces a novel curriculum-scheduled approach to contrastive training, addressing a gap in existing methods that fail to adapt augmentation strength dynamically. The innovation lies in systematically structuring training difficulty, which has not been thoroughly explored in the context of contrastive learning for text classification. This presents a significant advancement over previous methods like CERT.

#### Significance: 100/100
The findings are highly significant, particularly for low-resource text classification tasks, where access to large labelled datasets is often a barrier. CurCon's improvements over state-of-the-art methods suggest it could have a substantial impact on real-world applications. The reported gains in accuracy demonstrate that the method is not only effective but also practical for deployment in scenarios with limited labelled data.

#### Clarity: 100/100
The paper is exceptionally well-written and clearly structured. Each section logically progresses, with clear explanations of the methodology, experimental setup, results, and implications. The use of tables for presenting results enhances readability, and the ablation studies are articulated well, allowing readers to understand the contribution of each component.

### Final Average Score: 100/100

### Recommendation: Accept
The paper presents a well-rounded, innovative approach that significantly enhances low-resource text classification methods. Its sound experimental design, coupled with clear and concise presentation, makes it a valuable contribution to the field.
