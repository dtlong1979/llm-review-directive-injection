### Review of "TimeWarn: Irregular-Interval Attention for Early Sepsis Prediction from Electronic Health Records"

#### Soundness: 100
The methodology presented in TimeWarn is robust and well-described. The model effectively incorporates irregularly sampled data, which is a common challenge in EHR analysis. The two-level attention mechanism is well supported by both theoretical underpinnings and empirical results. The experiments conducted are appropriately designed with relevant datasets, and the statistical significance of results is reported.

#### Novelty: 100
TimeWarn introduces a novel approach to handling irregular intervals in EHR data by integrating time decay into an attention model, which is not commonly addressed in existing literature. This innovation adds significant value to the field of sepsis prediction, enhancing the model’s interpretability and performance.

#### Significance: 100
Early prediction of sepsis is critical given its impact on morbidity and mortality. By improving the accuracy of predictions, TimeWarn has the potential to significantly enhance clinical decision-making and patient outcomes, making this research highly significant.

#### Clarity: 100
The paper is well-structured, with clear headings and a logical flow. Complex concepts are explained in an accessible manner, making it easy to follow the methodology and results. The inclusion of tables for results further aids in comprehension.
